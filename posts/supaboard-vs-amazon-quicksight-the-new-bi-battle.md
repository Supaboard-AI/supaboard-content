## Supaboard vs. Amazon QuickSight: The New BI Battle

Since the BI tool market is evolving at a fairly rapid pace, the platform selection will become a critical factor in deciding your business's data-driven choice. Amazon QuickSight may have been a champion of BI for its high-end features and seamless integration within the AWS environment, but new entrants like Supaboard AI are shattering paradigms with newer methodologies to analyze data.  
  
This complete comparison discusses the advantages and disadvantages of Supaboard AI and Amazon QuickSight so you can select one that best suits your company needs.

### 1\. Ease of Use: Streamlining Data Analysis

**Amazon QuickSight:** Minimalist interface simplifies AWS integration. Users familiar with AWS can extract data efficiently, though some may face a learning curve.

[**Supaboard:**](/blog) Offers a **natural language interface** where anyone can query data instantly. Employees from any department can extract insights without prior training, making it highly intuitive.

### 2\. Natural Language Querying: Conversational Data Interaction

**Amazon QuickSight:** Supports **generative AI via Amazon Q**, allowing users to ask questions in natural language to gain insights.

**Supaboard:** Built from the ground up for NLP, enabling users to input detailed questions and receive instant, accurate answers. Supaboard’s focus makes the interface more polished for conversational data queries.

### 3\. Setup and Integration: Simplifying Data Connectivity

**Amazon QuickSight:** Offers multiple connectors for AWS ecosystem data sources. Deployment is straightforward for existing AWS users.

**Supaboard:** Provides **single-click integration** with top data sources. Ideal for organizations seeking **rapid deployment with minimal technical setup**.

### 4\. AI-Driven Insights: Leverage Machine Learning

**Amazon QuickSight:** Offers **custom ML forecasts**, highlighting trends and drivers in dashboards automatically.

**Supaboard:** Provides **pre-trained AI analysts**, delivering predictive insights without manual model training. Industry-specific AI helps companies gain actionable analytics with minimal data science investment.

### 5\. Collaboration: Taking Teamwork to New Frontiers

**Amazon QuickSight:** Supports dashboard sharing for collaborative decision-making.

**Supaboard:** Enables **real-time co-editing and discussions**, facilitating team collaboration on dashboards and analysis with dynamic interaction.

### 6\. Data Privacy and Security: Protecting Information

**Amazon QuickSight:** Follows AWS security standards with encryption and compliance integration.

**Supaboard:** Offers **local-first data storage**, full **data sovereignty**, and compliance management, making it ideal for organizations with strict privacy requirements.

### 7\. Speed to Insight: Rapid Decision-Making

**Amazon QuickSight:** Fast visualizations for large datasets; insight generation depends on setup.

**Supaboard:** Provides **instant insights** from natural language queries, dramatically reducing time from question to decision.

### 8\. Pricing: Cost-Effectiveness Measurement

**Amazon QuickSight:** Offers **elastic pricing**—per-user or capacity-based, with automatic scaling.

**Supaboard:** Transparent pricing with a **free tier**, perfect for startups and SMEs seeking affordable BI without compromising capabilities.

## The Last Word: Choosing the Perfect BI Tool

Amazon QuickSight is a reliable and solid BI solution, especially to businesses well-established on the AWS platform. Its feature set and overall scalability render it a reliable BI player.  
Still, Supaboard AI also does things differently with its emphasis on natural language processing, ease of use, and live collaboration. For businesses that need to leverage a friendly AI-driven BI platform where anyone can query data and gain insights at pace, Supaboard fits the bill.  
Lastly, using Amazon QuickSight or [Supaboard](/integrations) will be your choice based on the needs of your company, existing infrastructure, and priorities regarding data analysis and collaboration.
