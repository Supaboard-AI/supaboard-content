Yes. Supaboard is built for **startups, scaling SaaS teams, and enterprises**. Startups benefit from fast setup and no-code dashboards, while enterprises use Supaboard for scalable analytics, secure data access, and cross-team visibility without heavy BI infrastructure.

Stella AI is Supaboard’s built-in AI data assistant. It uses natural-language processing to understand business questions, analyze connected data, detect trends, and recommend actions automatically across your dashboards.
