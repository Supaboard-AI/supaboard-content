## Introduction: Business Decisions Need Fast Data, Not Frustration

You know the feeling: something shifts in your business sales drop, traffic spikes, or a campaign misses the mark and you need to understand why. But instead of getting answers right away, you’re left waiting for someone to dig up a report. Maybe it arrives tomorrow, maybe not. By then, the critical moment to respond has already passed.

This is a common frustration for teams today. The pace of business keeps accelerating, but too often the data tools we rely on can’t keep up. If you’ve ever wished for faster, clearer answers when it matters most, you’re not alone.

We tried them all: Tableau, Power BI, Metabase, Looker, Triple Whale. They’re solid in their own way   especially if you’ve got a data team.

But for business teams that move fast and don’t want to rely on someone else every time they need a number, they just don’t cut it.

Here’s what we kept running into:

-   Long setup times  
    
-   Confusing interfaces

And honestly? It felt like we were working for the tool, not the other way around.
