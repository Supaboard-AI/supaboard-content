## The Big Problem with Traditional Business Intelligence Tools

Today, businesses collect data from dozens of sources. CRM platforms are used by sales teams, marketing launches campaigns on different channels and operations keeps an eye on inventory in different systems. The result is data everywhere.

A lot of companies use tools like Metabase, Looker or Power BI. While these solutions work well for the technical user, they often leave non-technical team members waiting for reports. Business users struggle to get answers quickly and analysts spend hours writing queries.Common complaints include:

-   SQL is too time consuming
    
-   Dashboard overload that gets old fast
    
-   Trouble consolidating data from various tools
    
-   Ad-hoc questions lead to slower response times
    

We built [Supaboard AI](/) to solve these very problems. It lets anyone on the team ask questions in plain English and get back accurate, contextually relevant insights, without needing to write any code.
