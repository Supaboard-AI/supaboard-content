# Post writing and editing

For every post-related writing task in this project, run the `humanizer:humanizer`
skill before delivery. This includes blog posts, social posts, newsletters,
Substack and LinkedIn articles, and repurposed versions, whether new or revised.

Read the installed skill's `SKILL.md` and perform its full editing process:
identify patterns, rewrite, check the draft, and finalize. Preserve supported
facts, citations, link targets, product claims, metadata and image references.
Do not invent personal experience, introduce deliberate errors or claim that a
revision will pass an AI detector.

Run this pass after substantive drafting or SEO edits and before the final
render. Refresh affected previews and delivery bundles from the revised source.
Changing the writing workflow does not authorize publishing or editing unrelated
existing posts.
