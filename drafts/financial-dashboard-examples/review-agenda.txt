FINANCIAL DASHBOARD REVIEW AGENDA

1. Confirm the basis: period, snapshot date, entity, currency, definitions and freshness.
2. Review revenue and margin: what changed and which underlying records explain it?
3. Review collections: reconcile opening overdue to closing overdue using newly overdue amounts, receipts, credits and other adjustments.
4. Inspect priority records: verify notes and choose an action and owner for each.
5. Close the loop: set the next review date and the evidence required to show progress.
