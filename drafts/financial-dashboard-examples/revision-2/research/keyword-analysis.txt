4. Live SERP and intent
The live query financial dashboard examples showed examples/templates from Qlik, dashboard design inspiration from Dribbble, a ZoomCharts Power BI template, Visbanking, a Power BI report, Embark and Klipfolio in the seven organic results returned. Result types included AI Overview, images, video, People Also Ask and related searches. People Also Ask included What is a financial dashboard? and Is there a free Excel template for creating a financial dashboard?

This supports a concrete example and a reusable comparison table. It does not justify turning the article into an Excel tutorial, personal-finance guide or exhaustive vendor list. The revised article delivers profitability, receivables and actual-cash-flow distinctions through one coherent business problem.

Qlik's and Klipfolio's primary pages were opened. Klipfolio organizes examples by CFO, revenue, cash flow, expense and P&L views and lists relevant KPIs. Our proposed distinction is editorial: connect a green performance view to the omitted overdue question, reconcile the change, and show the invoice-level action. This is a specific contribution, not a claim that no competitor has ever covered collections.

5. AI result observations and choices
The live AI Overview cited Qlik, Visbanking, Power BI, ThoughtSpot, Klipfolio, Geckoboard and others. OpenRush's separate sampled category citation map included sources such as Qlik, NetSuite, Tableau, Databox and ThoughtSpot. That map covers Google AI Overviews only and is broader than one live query; its counts are not citation probabilities, total market share, or evidence of ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity visibility.

The revision makes the central answer explicit near the opening, provides a self-contained profit-versus-cash explanation with an SEC citation, defines the receivables aging basis, uses semantic table headers, labels every example as fictional, and places product claims next to the relevant product link. These choices improve clarity and retrievability. No ranking, snippet or AI citation is promised.
