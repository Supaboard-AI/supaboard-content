We were quite comfortable with how we’d organised Supaboard. Each feature had a place, and the structure made sense to us as builders.

What made us question it was watching how people moved through a problem.

They’d investigate something in their data, ask follow-up questions, and only then decide what needed ongoing attention. The dashboard or weekly report grew out of that conversation.

That changed the question we were building around: how could we carry that understanding into what someone wanted to do next?

My cofounder Aritra wrote about the shift, the pilot, and the customer feedback that shaped it.
