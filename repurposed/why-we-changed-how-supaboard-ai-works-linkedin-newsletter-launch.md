Supaboard’s original layout made sense to us as builders. Watching people use it gave us a reason to rethink it.

Someone would start by investigating a revenue drop, narrow it down to a customer segment, then want a dashboard or weekly report to keep tracking it. They were figuring out what to monitor through the conversation.

That raised a question for us: could the AI carry that context through the whole workflow?

In our latest newsletter article, our cofounder Aritra shares why we changed how Supaboard’s AI works, how we tested the idea with a customer, and how feedback shaped what we built.
