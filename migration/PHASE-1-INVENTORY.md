## What `inferredTargetQuery` is and is not

`inventory.json` records an **inferred** target query per post, derived from the
slug. It is a clustering signal for Phase 2 only. The authored `targetQuery` of
the Phase 3 schema is a different field and must be written by a human — the
brief is explicit that a post which cannot supply one belongs in KILL or MERGE.
